Brother Super PowerNote PN-8500MDS

Computing & Writing

← Back to collection

A dedicated meeting of keyboard, screen and the written page.

With its hinged display and Turkish-character keyboard, the Brother Super PowerNote PN-8500MDS presents a workspace centred on writing. Brother’s history in this field runs from the EP-20 electronic printer of 1982 to the Picoword word processor introduced in 1984. This piece carries that tradition’s quiet, functional design language: a working experience in which the keyboard directs attention straight towards the text.
