Privacy

Privacy and analytics choices

This site uses Google Analytics 4 to understand visits and improve its content only after you give explicit permission.

Before you consent

The Google Analytics tag is not downloaded, no analytics request is sent and no analytics cookie is created. Your choice is stored only in your browser’s local storage.

When you consent

Google Analytics may process usage data such as the page viewed, approximate location, device and browser type, referral source and page interactions. This site does not enable advertising storage or personalised advertising.

Changing your choice

You can change your choice at any time using the “Cookie preferences” button at the bottom-right of the page. When you reject analytics, analytics storage is denied and Google Analytics cookies accessible to this site are removed.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact mail@cagricakir.com.tr. Learn more about Google’s processing in the Google Privacy Policy.
