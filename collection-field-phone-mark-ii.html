Field Phone Mark II

Computing & Writing

← Back to collection

A telephone drawing on the visual language of earlier communication equipment.

The Field Phone Mark II brings the visual language of earlier field communications into a retro desktop telephone. Spain’s official gazette records an S.O.S.L. Field Phone Mark II accepted in December 1997, placing the name within late-1990s design culture. Its angular case, handset and decorative keypad translate the strong character of historical communication equipment into a domestic object.
