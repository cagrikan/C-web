Facit Mechanical Calculator for Jewellers

Computing & Writing

← Back to collection

A Facit that turns numbers into keys, levers and geared movement, with a history of use in jewellery work.

This Facit mechanical calculator shows how arithmetic became a tangible working rhythm before electronic displays. Number windows, keys, red function buttons and the side crank turn repeated calculations into a sequence of deliberate hand movements. The owner-reported history of use in jewellery work, together with the Said Erim, Galata dealer plate, adds a local layer from Istanbul’s commercial life.
