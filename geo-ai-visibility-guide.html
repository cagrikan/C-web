GEO · AI visibility

What is Generative Engine Optimization?

GEO is the combined work used to help a brand, person, product or service be understood, mentioned, cited or recommended accurately and currently in answers generated by ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini and similar systems.

Published: 10 July 2026 · Updated: 30 October 2026

The concise answer

GEO combines entity clarity, technical accessibility, structured data, clear content, evidence and third-party sources to improve how AI systems understand and represent an entity.

Traditional search presents a list of links. Generative systems often produce a direct answer. Visibility is therefore no longer only the position of a page; it also concerns which entity appears in the answer and how it is described.

GEO and SEO

SEO improves visibility in search results through crawlability, indexing, page quality, authority and user intent. GEO shares many of those foundations, but asks a different set of questions:

  • Which category does the system associate with the brand?
  • For which questions is the brand mentioned or omitted?
  • Which sources support the answer?
  • Is the narrative accurate and current?
  • Are the brand’s claims corroborated by independent sources?

SEO and GEO are not opponents. A sound web, entity and content structure supports both. GEO measurement, however, extends beyond ranking and clicks into mention, citation, recommendation and narrative accuracy.

How do AI systems understand a brand?

1. Entity clarity

Brand name, people, products, services, categories, locations and relationships should remain consistent across the site and external sources. Conflicting descriptions weaken confidence.

2. Technical foundations

Pages should be accessible, use correct canonical and hreflang relationships, appear in the sitemap and carry structured data that matches visible content. Crawler permissions may enable access, but do not guarantee that a system will use the content.

3. Clear, answerable content

Explain who the service is for, which problem it solves, the method used and what is delivered. Definitions, comparisons, Q&A and process content are easier to understand than vague marketing slogans.

4. Evidence and authority

Experience, methods, cases, publications, expertise and independent references support claims. Statements repeated only by the brand itself remain weak evidence.

5. Freshness and consistency

Changes to services, teams, pricing models or expertise should be updated consistently across the site, profiles and other sources.

How is AI visibility measured?

  1. Define a target question set: brand, category, problem, comparison and purchase-intent questions.
  2. Establish a baseline: test the same questions in selected systems and record the answers.
  3. Classify indicators: mention, citation, recommendation, share of voice and narrative accuracy.
  4. Implement actions: improve entity, technical, content and authority gaps.
  5. Measure again using the same method: changing prompts and evaluation rules destroys comparability.

A single screenshot is not evidence of success. Generative systems are variable. The meaningful signal is improvement across repeated measurement and a more accurate narrative over time.

What does llms.txt do?

llms.txt is an optional text file that summarises a site for machine consumers that choose to use it. It can provide an organised statement of identity, services and important pages. It is not a universal standard, ranking factor or visibility guarantee, and is not a complete GEO implementation by itself.

Why is GEO continuous?

The brand, competitors, web sources and AI systems all change. Baselines, technical structure, content, evidence and measurement must be refreshed. One-off optimisation loses value as information becomes stale.

Frequently asked questions

What is GEO?

The technical, content and authority work used to help an entity be understood, mentioned, cited or recommended accurately in AI answers.

How is GEO different from SEO?

SEO focuses on visibility and clicks in search results. GEO focuses on accurate understanding and representation within the direct answer generated by AI.

How is AI visibility measured?

The same target questions are tested regularly, and mention, citation, recommendation, share of voice and narrative accuracy are tracked over time.

Are schema or llms.txt sufficient?

No. They must be combined with clear content, source consistency, crawlability, evidence, third-party authority and recurring measurement.
