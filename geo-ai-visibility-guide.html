What Is Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)? How Brands Become Visible in AI Engines

GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) is the discipline of ensuring a brand is accurately, currently, and visibly represented by AI search engines such as ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, and Gemini. Below, drawing on my experience as a corporate technology investor in geoni.ai, I explain what GEO is and how it works.

What Is Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)?

Short answer: GEO is the discipline of ensuring a brand or individual appears accurately, currently, and visibly in the answers generated by AI engines. It works through structured data, content clarity, and machine-readable sources.

For years, search engines directed users toward a list of links. AI engines now generate direct answers instead — a user can become aware of a brand, product, or person without ever clicking through to a website. This fundamentally changes what "visibility" means.

The key difference between GEO and SEO

SEO aims to rank a page higher in search results and get the user to click through. GEO goes a step further: it aims to ensure a brand is accurately cited, summarized, or recommended within the answer an AI engine generates. In some cases, the user encounters the brand without visiting the site at all.

How Does a Brand Become Visible in AI Engines?

Visibility isn't reducible to a single technical setting — it emerges from several layers working together. The steps below are the foundational building blocks of GEO:

1. Add structured data

JSON-LD markup following the Schema.org standard helps AI models clearly understand who, what, and why a page exists. Schema types like Person, Organization, and FAQPage play a critical role here.

2. Grant access to AI bots

Explicitly allowing AI crawlers such as GPTBot, ClaudeBot, and PerplexityBot in robots.txt is a prerequisite for your content to be crawled by these engines at all.

3. Provide a clear summary via llms.txt

llms.txt is a file that summarizes who a site belongs to, what it does, and which pages matter, in markdown format. It directly contributes to AI assistants summarizing the site accurately.

4. Signal freshness

Visible update dates and dateModified fields within structured data show that content is current. AI engines place less trust in content that appears stale.

5. Produce clear question-and-answer content

AI engines prefer to cite content that answers a clear question directly, with bulleted and well-structured formatting. This very page is designed according to that principle.

Why Is GEO an Uninterrupted Process, Not a One-Time Setup?

A common mistake here is treating GEO as a one-time "installation." In reality, AI engines periodically re-crawl content and their models are continuously updated, while the competitive landscape keeps shifting. This is why GEO should not be designed like a traditional, now-outdated one-off enterprise software rollout — it should be designed as an uninterrupted, continuity-focused, and sustainable critical system. Much like a building's electrical or water infrastructure — it isn't something you set up and forget, but a living system that requires ongoing maintenance and monitoring.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between GEO and SEO?

SEO aims to rank a website higher on search engine results pages and get users to click through. GEO aims to ensure a brand is accurately cited, summarized, or recommended within the direct answers generated by AI engines.

What does an llms.txt file do?

llms.txt summarizes who a website belongs to, what it does, and which pages matter, in markdown format, for AI assistants. It helps AI models summarize the site accurately and consistently.

Why should GEO be an uninterrupted, sustainable process?

AI engines periodically re-crawl content and their models get updated; a one-time optimization loses its effect over time. GEO should be designed as an uninterrupted, continuously maintained system rather than a one-off project.
