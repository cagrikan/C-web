Zenit 11 with Helios-44M-4

Photography & Cinema

← Back to collection

An analogue pairing that places framing, focus and exposure in the photographer’s hands.

The Zenit 11 with Helios-44M-4 foregrounds photography as a deliberate relationship with controls. The manufacturer’s archive describes the Zenit 11 as a development intended to replace the Zenit-EM, while the manual lists the Helios-44M-4 among its supplied lens options. Documented with a 58 mm focal length and f/2 maximum aperture, the lens is a classic companion to the camera’s direct, hands-on design.
