Revox B77

Analogue reel-to-reel tape recorder · Sound & Music

← Back to collection

Reels, meters and direct controls make the path of sound visible.

The Revox B77 makes recorded sound’s relationship with physical material unusually clear: reels, tape path and controls can be read together across its face. Revox’s own account places the B77 in a lineage reaching back to the 1970s. This example’s large reels and twin meters draw attention to recording as a process, not simply playback as an outcome. Its appeal lies in that visible interaction rather than an unverified promise about sound quality. The precise version, tape speeds and track configuration remain unconfirmed; the contents of the pictured reels and their inclusion in a sale also require separate confirmation.
