Bauer Film Projector

Photography & Cinema

← Back to collection

The mechanical route a moving image takes before reaching the screen.

This Bauer projector invites attention to cinema as a mechanism as well as an image. The brand belongs to Stuttgart’s film-projection tradition, reflected in a Bauer mechanism dated 1907–1908 in the TECHNOSEUM collection and in Bosch archives covering professional projection and amateur filmmaking. Its housing, lens and film transport turn the mechanical work behind the screen into part of the object’s appeal.
